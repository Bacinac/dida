Cameras belong to **BABA**, DIDA's sibling — the vision system. DIDA subscribes to what BABA sees and turns it into ordinary entities: motion, occupancy, a count of people or vehicles, a recognised person. [Rules](/help/automations) use those like any other reading.

Every stream goes through DIDA's own API, by entity. A browser only ever talks to DIDA: the video rides the same login and the same tunnel as the rest of the app, and no camera is exposed publicly.

The Cameras page shows the live grid. A [notification](/help/notifications) triggered by a camera can carry the frame that caused it.

More than one BABA installation can feed one DIDA — a second house, a holiday place — and each keeps its own cameras and credentials.
