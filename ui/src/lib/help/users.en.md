There are two roles: **admin** and **user**. Automations and most of Settings are for admins only.

For a user, an admin decides:

- which pages they may see,
- whether they may control devices at all (a view-only user cannot).

That baseline can be overridden for a single entity with a **control grant**, and view rules can hide individual entities from a user. A user given only [Entry](/help/entry) gets exactly that page and nothing else.

Users are login accounts. They are not the same as the people the house tracks for [presence](/help/presence): a person is someone the house knows the whereabouts of, a user is someone who can sign in.
